About

I'm Nirazan Pandey, a photographer working across portraits, landscapes and events. I'm also the team photographer for Southern Knights CC, covering match days, training and club life through the season.

My approach stays out of the way of the moment. I spend the first few minutes of any session just watching — how someone stands, how light falls across a room, where a game turns — and only then start shooting. The best frames are usually the ones nobody was posing for.

Whether it's a quiet portrait session, a wedding, or a Saturday afternoon at the ground, the goal is the same: images that still feel true years from now.
